Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · Brand: Anandavan Tunes

Anandavan Tunes respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our website and internal creator publishing tools handle information when connecting to official third-party platforms (TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram).

1. Information We Collect

  • Public Website Visitors: We do not collect personal information, run third-party tracking cookies, or sell visitor data on this static website.
  • Authorized Creator Accounts Only: When the owner of Anandavan Tunes explicitly authorizes our publishing application via OAuth 2.0, the application receives an access token, optional refresh token, and basic account identifier (such as a YouTube Channel ID, TikTok open_id, or Instagram Business Account ID) solely to verify the target account and upload creator-owned videos.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Anandavan Tunes produces family-friendly and child-directed educational and musical content. We do not knowingly collect, store, or share personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Our publishing tools operate exclusively for adult channel administrators.

3. How We Use Connected Platform APIs

  • TikTok API: Scopes such as user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish are used strictly to verify the authorized creator account and upload/draft or publish original Anandavan Tunes videos. We do not collect or store data about other TikTok users.
  • YouTube API Services: Used strictly to upload original videos, set titles/descriptions/thumbnails, assign playlists, and apply Made-for-Kids and synthetic media disclosures. Please also review the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram Graph API: Scopes such as instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish are used strictly to publish original 9:16 Reels to the authorized Anandavan Tunes Business/Creator account.

4. Data Storage, Sharing & Security

OAuth credentials and publishing logs are stored privately on the channel owner's secured environment. We never sell, rent, or transfer OAuth tokens or account data to third parties.

5. Data Deletion & Revoking Access

Authorized account owners may revoke access and delete stored tokens at any time:

  • TikTok: Open TikTok Settings → Security and login → Manage app permissions → remove Anandavan Tunes.
  • Google / YouTube: Revoke access at Google Account Permissions.
  • Instagram / Meta: Open Instagram Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → remove Anandavan Tunes.
  • Direct Deletion Request: Email contact@anandavantunes.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request” and we will purge any stored tokens or logs within 48 hours.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Information

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: contact@anandavantunes.com.
